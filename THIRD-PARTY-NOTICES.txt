===== RegesARC nebula provenance =====

RegesARC desktop nebula

The desktop effect adapts the existing original RegesARC website's particle simulation and integration. The same curl-noise simulation, life-cycle and central orb provide a bounded desktop presentation; it is rendered locally without remote images, scripts or services.

Third-party components and complete license texts:
- Three.js and its GPUComputationRenderer utility: node_modules/three/LICENSE, copied to the release notices by prepare-assets.mjs.
- Ashima / Stefan Gustavson WebGL noise: build/licenses/webgl-noise-MIT.txt.
- Samsy's historical simplex derivative routine, used by the website reference: build/licenses/samsy-derivative-noise-MIT.txt. Source: https://samsy.ninja/nebula/index.html . The website's source ledger is retained separately under website/vendor/.

The GLSL sources are byte-identical to the audited website copies:
- noise4D.glsl SHA-256: cf936fb1a8a987568ef8a8ad01e6b60bb4baddfbb9809dd10f47ebd5aae65f83
- simplexNoiseDerivatives.glsl SHA-256: ce4d8b6d234787aff0164f42ebc28021ae42de7d6da6d9be85678027ff860383

The static executable icon is an actual locally rendered frame from the RegesARC nebula component, exported as a 256 × 256 PNG and embedded unchanged in the Windows ICO. It is not a screenshot copied from a third-party site.
